Drag the cube to inspect all 6 faces. Each turn: 1) click an empty sticker to place your mark, then 2) click a move button to rotate one cube layer. Standard Rubik's notation: U=Up, D=Down, L=Left, R=Right, F=Front, B=Back. Apostrophe (') means counter-clockwise (when looking at that face from outside). Win by getting 3 marks in a row, column, or diagonal on a single face — checked after the rotation completes.
